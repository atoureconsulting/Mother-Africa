Mother Africa Festival 2026 · Creator proposition

AToure · 08

On both nights we run a small Creator Hub on site. It is where creators check in, charge their phones, edit and post, and where our content producer keeps them on the moments that matter — headline sets, sponsor activations, the crowd at its peak. Access to stage-side and backstage areas is timed and agreed with production in advance, so creators never get in the way of the show. We need a small space and power from the venue; we will confirm the footprint with production.
