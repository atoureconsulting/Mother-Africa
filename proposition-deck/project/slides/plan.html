AToure · 13

The plan assumes we start on Monday 2 November. Three weeks to scout, vet and contract creators; briefing and the content calendar overlap with the end of that. Build-up content runs from 23 November to Boxing Day, while accreditation and the hub are set up in December. Then the two festival nights, the afterglow into January, and the final report by mid-January. Every week we start later compresses the build-up, which is where tickets are sold.
