## Found but left off the slide

- **Organiser:** KT7 Production; Kimany Tayoro named as founder and president
  ([Pulse](https://www.pulse.ci/article/mother-africa-donne-pour-sa-5e-edition-le-festival-sengage-pour-leducation-des-jeunes-2026100916352580778),
  [allAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610100163.html),
  [Vanguard](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2024/05/kimany-tayoro-founder-of-mother-africa-festival/)).
  Two other sources credit Fontaine Traoré, and a 2022 report highlights Joël
  Williams of MLC Agency. Do not name a founder in the deck until confirmed.
- **Mother Africa donne:** the festival's solidarity programme. 140 school kits
  given to the national girls' orphanage in Grand Bassam on 8 October 2026
  (Pulse, allAfrica). A good line to open the meeting with.
- **Tickets:** from 10,000 FCFA in 2024 (one source).
- **2022 forecast:** organisers expected 40,000+ over three days; about 15,000
  came. Do not quote the forecast.
- **Founded:** 2022 in Abidjan.

## Conflicts to watch

- **2026 dates:** the poster and Tikerama say 27 and 28 December; Pulse (9
  October 2026) says 26 and 27 December. The deck follows the poster.
- **2025 lineup:** some listings also name Aya Nakamura, Rema, Fally Ipupa and
  others for 2025. The slide uses only the names the press launch coverage
  agrees on (Gims, Asake).
- **No published attendance for 2023, 2024 or 2025.** Ask the festival for
  their figures: they would make slide 2 stronger than any public number.
