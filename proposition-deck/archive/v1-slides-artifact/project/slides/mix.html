Mother Africa Festival 2026 · Creator proposition

AToure · 05

We build the roster in four tiers, because each does a different job. Headline creators bring reach and own the big announcements. Culture creators give credibility with the people who actually go out in Abidjan. Community creators are where trust and ticket sales happen, each with a personal code. Diaspora creators reach the people deciding whether to spend December at home. The numbers in brackets are a starting point; we size them to the budget. Every creator is vetted for real audiences and brand safety before we sign them.
