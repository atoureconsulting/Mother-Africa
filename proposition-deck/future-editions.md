# Ideas kept for a future edition

These were in the first version of the proposal and were taken off the
slides for the 5th edition pitch. They are kept here to bring back for a
later edition, a retainer, or a bigger scope. The original slide files are in
`archive/v1-slides-artifact/project/slides/` (`mix.html`, `manage.html`,
`hub.html`, `invest.html`).

## 1. Four tiers of creators

A roster built in four tiers, because each tier does a different job. The
numbers were a starting point to size against the budget.

| Tier | Suggested number | Who | What they bring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline creators | 3 to 5 | Large Ivorian and West African audiences | Reach, and the big moments: lineup and headliner reveals |
| Culture creators | 8 to 10 | Music, fashion, food and nightlife voices in Abidjan | Credibility with fans: looks, food, the night |
| Community creators | 15 to 20 | Micro creators, students and superfans | Trust, and ticket sales through personal codes |
| Diaspora creators | 3 to 5 | Ivorian and African voices in Paris, Brussels, London, Montréal | Reach the diaspora deciding whether to spend December at home |

Every creator is vetted for real audiences and brand safety before signing.

## 2. Pay and close (step 6 of creator management)

The last step of the end to end process:

- Creators paid on proof of delivery, not on appearance.
- Content rights logged so the festival and its partners can reuse the best
  pieces.

## 3. A Creator Hub at Marcory Zone 4

A small on site base for creators on both festival nights:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Creator desk | Check in, wristbands and the night's run of show |
| Content producer | Spots the moments that matter and points creators to them |
| Charge and upload | A charging point and a quiet corner to edit and post |
| Timed access | Stage side and backstage windows agreed with production |
| Sponsor route | Creators guided through every partner activation |
| Fast approvals | Quick checks so posts go out while the moment is live |

Needs a small space and power from the venue; confirm the footprint with
production.

## 4. Investment: three packages

Management fee separate from creator fees, which pass through at cost so the
festival sees exactly what each creator is paid. Fees were left blank.

| Package | Scope |
|---|---|
| Essential | About 10 creators (culture and community tiers), full creator management, build up and festival nights, final report |
| Signature (the recommended one) | About 25 creators across all four tiers, full creator management with the on site Creator Hub, all three waves including afterglow, weekly pulse, nightly recaps, final report with sponsor pages |
| Anniversary | About 40 creators with more headline and diaspora voices, everything in Signature, dedicated creator content for two sponsors, an edited content library with rights cleared for the following year |

Pricing for the trial, the retainer and the report on its own is still an
open question in `offers/creator-visibility-offer/pitch-playbook.md` (AToure
repo).
